## What Is Data Science to Me?

To me, data science is really about using numbers and information to understand problems and find better solutions. I have always been a big math person, especially when it comes to statistics. I naturally like looking at numbers, comparing things, and using data to back up my thoughts. My friends and family will even joke, “Here he comes with the stats,” because I am always bringing numbers into the conversation. Because of that, data science feels like a field that fits me naturally.

I also have always been interested in technology. I started my journey in Computer Science at a junior college in California, and now I am continuing that path while also minoring in Economics. I like that data science allows me to bring all of those interests together. I can use programming, statistics, economics, and technology to actually solve problems instead of just learning each subject separately.

Right now, I am learning how to work with Python, Pandas, NumPy, Excel, statistics, and data visualization. I am still learning and figuring out what areas of data science I enjoy the most, but I know that I like business and financial analytics, sports analytics, and finding ways to make data easier to understand through visualization.

What I like most about data science is how many different directions I can take it. I could use it in finance because I have always been interested in capital and how money moves. I could use it in sports because sports have been a major part of my life. I could even use it with animals or marine life, which are fields I have been interested in since before I chose Computer Science.

Overall, data science gives me a way to combine a lot of the things I enjoy. I am still learning, but I am motivated to keep improving and see where I can take these skills.
